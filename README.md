# Stranger Things 🚲🧇

> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven

**Stranger Things** is a thrilling science-fiction horror drama series created by the Duffer Brothers for Netflix. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, it follows a group of kids as they uncover supernatural mysteries and government secrets.

## Main Characters

* **Eleven** (Jane Hopper) - Possesses psychokinetic and telepathic abilities.
* **Mike Wheeler** - The loyal and determined leader of the group.
* **Dustin Henderson** - The tech-savvy, witty, and lovable science enthusiast.
* **Lucas Sinclair** - Pragmatic and skilled with a wrist rocket.
* **Will Byers** - Whose mysterious disappearance kicks off the entire story.
